08FAQ

What peopleask us most

How much does a bespoke quartz worktop cost?

Kitchen worktops start from €290/m². The final price depends on material, thickness, edges, cut-outs and installation. Send approximate sizes and we’ll price it within 24 hours.

Do you work across the whole Alicante province?

Yes: Alicante, Elche, Novelda, Benidorm, Torrevieja, Jávea, Dénia and the whole Costa Blanca. Murcia and Valencia on request.

How long from survey to installation?

It depends on the project and the material. We give you the exact lead time before we start and we stick to it.

Can I visit the workshop to choose the slab?

Of course. We are in Novelda; book a visit by phone or WhatsApp and we’ll show you the slabs in stock.

Do you cut material I bring myself?

Yes. We cut, profile, make cut-outs, polish and waterjet-cut your own material.

Do you speak English?

Yes. We work in English, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian.
